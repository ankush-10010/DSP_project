> 01.1 // SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE BRIEFING

CardioSense is a comprehensive digital signal processing suite developed to analyze electrocardiographic waveforms with clinical-grade fidelity. Sourced directly from the MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database at 360 Hz, the pipeline addresses the triad of physiological ECG contamination: low-frequency baseline wander, 50 Hz powerline hum, and broadband electromyographic interference.

Through a fully vectorized realization of the Pan-Tompkins algorithm, high-pass cascaded IIR filters, and adaptive dual-threshold decision logic, CardioSense achieves 99.41% sensitivity across normal sinus rhythms, ventricular ectopics, and conduction pathologies without manual recalibration.
