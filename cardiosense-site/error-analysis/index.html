// WHY PVCs ARE HARDER TO DETECT

The Pan-Tompkins algorithm relies on an explicit physiological assumption: that depolarization proceeds through the high-velocity His-Purkinje specialized conduction system. Normal supraventricular depolarizations (and Atrial Premature Contractions) depolarize the ventricles at 2.0 to 4.0 meters per second, generating tight, narrow QRS complexes (<100 ms duration) with steep spectral energy concentrated exactly in the 8–16 Hz band.

Conversely, Premature Ventricular Contractions originate directly in ventricular myocardium, bypassing the Purkinje network. Electrical wavefronts propagate cell-to-cell via gap junctions at a sluggish velocity of only 0.3 to 1.0 m/s. This broadens the complex (>140 ms) and shifts dominant frequency downward into the 1–4 Hz band.

Because the 5–15 Hz bandpass filter violently attenuates energy below 5 Hz, the true derivative slope dV/dt of a PVC is suppressed up to 3-fold. When fed into the non-linear squaring operator, this amplitude drop is squared into an 8- to 10-fold reduction in integrated energy, plunging the peak beneath the running adaptive signal threshold.

// CLINICAL IMPLICATIONS

RISKS OF FALSE POSITIVES (OVERSENSING)

SEVERITY: HIGH
  • FALSE TACHYCARDIA ALARMS: Double-counting ST-T or prominent pre-systolic deflections artificially doubles the calculated instantaneous heart rate (e.g., reporting 140 BPM instead of a true 70 BPM sinus rhythm), triggering urgent hemodynamic alerts.
  • CLINICAL ALARM FATIGUE: ICU audits demonstrate that >85% of telemetry alarms are non-actionable false positives. Persistent oversensing degrades nursing alertness, directly delaying interventions during true emergencies.
  • INAPPROPRIATE ICD DISCHARGES: In implantable cardioverter-defibrillator logic, double-sensing rapid depolarization transients misclassifies stable rhythm as ventricular fibrillation, delivering agonizing 30–40 joule shocks to conscious patients.

RISKS OF FALSE NEGATIVES (UNDERSENSING)

SEVERITY: CRITICAL
  • UNDERESTIMATED PVC BURDEN: Missed ectopics undercount premature ventricular burden, obscuring PVC-induced cardiomyopathy indications where clinical intervention requires >10% to 15% 24-hour ectopic prevalence.
  • UNDETECTED LETHAL RUNS: Failure to recognize low-amplitude polymorphic ventricular beats allows couplets, triplets, or runs of non-sustained ventricular tachycardia (NSVT) to pass unnoticed before degenerating into fatal VF.
  • DISTORTED HRV BIOMARKERS: Dropping normal or ectopic intervals artificially creates massive instantaneous RR pauses, completely corrupting autonomic HRV metrics (SDNN, RMSSD, LF/HF spectral ratio).
