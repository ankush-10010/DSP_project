> 03.1 // CONTAMINANT CHARACTERIZATION MATRIX

[NOH-740 STANDARDS COMPLIANT]
[CONTAMINANT_01] < 0.5 HZ

BASELINE WANDER

Frequency Domain: < 0.5 Hz (DC to Sub-Hertz drift)
Biophysical Origin:

Respiration, thoracic expansion, perspiration-induced electrode electrolyte impedance shift, electrode-skin half-cell potential drift.

Diagnostic Consequence:

Distorts isoelectric baseline, mimics acute ST-elevation or false depression, destabilizes adaptive QRS detection thresholds.

REMEDIATION ARCHITECTURE:

4th-order Butterworth High-Pass Filter (fc = 0.5 Hz) realized via zero-phase forward-backward recursive filtering.

[CONTAMINANT_02] 50.0 / 60.0 HZ

POWERLINE INTERFERENCE

Frequency Domain: 50.0 Hz (European) / 60.0 Hz (US mains)
Biophysical Origin:

230V/110V AC electrical infrastructure radiating magnetic fields, capacitive displacement currents in patient cables, ground loops.

Diagnostic Consequence:

Dense periodic sinusoidal ripple masking low-amplitude atrial P-waves, septal Q-deflections, and notched bundle-branch morphologies.

REMEDIATION ARCHITECTURE:

2nd-order IIR Digital Notch Biquad (f0 = 50.0 Hz, Q = 30.0, -3dB bandwidth 1.67 Hz) with rejection depth exceeding 42.8 dB.

[CONTAMINANT_03] > 100 HZ

MUSCLE TREMOR (EMG)

Frequency Domain: > 100 Hz (Broadband spectrum 20 - 500 Hz)
Biophysical Origin:

Somatic motor unit action potentials (MUAP), patient thermal shivering, postural micro-adjustments, myoclonic twitching.

Diagnostic Consequence:

High slew-rate stochastic spikes masquerade as narrow R-peaks, catastrophically inflating differentiation stage derivatives.

REMEDIATION ARCHITECTURE:

4th-order Butterworth Low-Pass Filter (fc = 150.0 Hz, -24 dB/octave roll-off) for broadband somatic isolation.
